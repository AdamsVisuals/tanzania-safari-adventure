10-Day Tanzania Safari & Zanzibar Adventure

Embark on an unforgettable 10-day adventure combining Tanzania's most iconic northern circuit parks with an immersive Zanzibar experience. This comprehensive journey takes you from the vibrant town of Arusha to Tarangire National Park, the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater, the breathtaking Serengeti plains, and concludes with cultural exploration and relaxation in Zanzibar.

Experience exceptional game viewing opportunities with chances to spot the Big Five, along with immersive cultural experiences in Zanzibar including Stone Town exploration, blue safaris, and sunset dhow cruises. Enjoy comfortable accommodations, expert guided game drives, and the perfect blend of wildlife adventure and beach relaxation.

Day 1: Arrive Arusha

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), you will be met by your driver-guide who will warmly welcome you. After a brief introduction, you'll be transferred to your accommodation in Arusha. Once you arrive, you can relax and enjoy a delicious dinner at your lodge. In the evening, a pre-safari briefing will be conducted, where your guide will provide an overview of the upcoming adventure, detailing the itinerary and important safari tips. This will be your opportunity to ask any questions and ensure you're fully prepared for the exciting days ahead. Afterward, you'll have a restful overnight stay at your lodge, ready to embark on your safari journey the following day.

Day 2: Arusha – Full Game drives in Tarangire National Park - Karatu

You will depart early in the morning from your lodge in Arusha and head towards Tarangire National Park for an exciting game drive. The journey covers approximately 120 km and will take about 2 hours. Packed lunch will be provided, allowing you to enjoy a meal while taking in the stunning wildlife surrounding you. Tarangire is renowned for its large herds of elephants, striking baobab trees, and diverse wildlife, including lions, giraffes, and various bird species. The park is particularly impressive during the dry season when animals gather around the Tarangire River. After an exhilarating day of exploration, you'll check out in the late afternoon and begin your transfer to the Rift Valley Escarpment Wall. This scenic route offers breathtaking views of the landscape, leading you to the Crater Highlands, where you'll arrive for dinner and an overnight stay at your lodge. The drive from Tarangire to the Crater Highlands is approximately 150 km and takes about 3 hours.

Day 3: Karatu – Full Game drives in Ngorongoro Crater – Karatu

After an early breakfast, depart for Ngorongoro Crater. Upon arrival, you'll begin your descent into the crater floor, which is often referred to as the "Garden of Eden." The crater is a UNESCO World Heritage site and home to an incredible variety of wildlife, including lions, elephants, rhinos, buffaloes, and various bird species. This volcanic caldera offers a unique opportunity to see a wide array of animals in one location, thanks to its diverse habitats ranging from grasslands to forested areas. You'll spend several hours on game drives, soaking in the natural beauty and abundant wildlife. In the late afternoon, you will ascend back out of the crater and head to your lodge in the Crater Highlands for a relaxing evening, where you'll enjoy dinner and an overnight stay.

Day 4: Karatu – Half Game drives in Central Serengeti

After an early breakfast, we depart from the Crater Highlands and make our way to the central part of Serengeti National Park. The journey covers approximately 150 kilometers and takes around 4 to 5 hours, including game viewing along the way. Our route passes through the stunning Ngorongoro Conservation Area, offering breathtaking landscapes and a chance to spot wildlife even before reaching Serengeti. With packed lunches provided, we continue the drive as the scenery transitions from the highlands to the endless plains of the Serengeti. Upon entering the park, we begin game drives en route, witnessing the incredible diversity of wildlife that the Serengeti is famous for, including large herds of wildebeest, zebras, predators, and a variety of bird species. By late afternoon, we arrive at your special camp or lodge in the central Serengeti, where you will enjoy dinner and an overnight stay under the vast African skies.

Day 5: Full Game drives in Central Serengeti

After breakfast, you will spend the entire day on full game drives in Serengeti National Park, one of the most famous wildlife reserves in the world. Central Serengeti is known for its rich wildlife and diverse landscapes, including vast savannahs, open plains, and riverine forests. It's a prime location to spot the "Big Five" (lions, elephants, leopards, buffalo, and rhinos) and is especially renowned for its lion population. During your full-day game drives, you will have ample opportunities to witness the incredible diversity of animals, such as cheetahs, giraffes, zebras, and wildebeest, as well as stunning birdlife. You will enjoy the day on full board basis, with breakfast, lunch, and dinner provided at your camp or a scenic spot in the park. Your full game drives will give you the opportunity to explore different regions of Central Serengeti, maximizing wildlife sightings and the chance to capture unforgettable photos. In the evening, you'll return to the camp to relax, enjoy dinner, and rest overnight. After a fulfilling day of adventure, we will return to our accommodation in the Serengeti for dinner and overnight, where you can relax and reflect on the day's experiences.

Day 6: Central Serengeti – Zanzibar

After an early breakfast and checkout from your lodge, you will be transferred to the Seronera Airstrip for your flight to Zanzibar. Upon arrival in Zanzibar, you will be greeted by your driver and transferred to your beach accommodation, where you can relax and enjoy the stunning coastal views. After checking in, you can unwind and enjoy the tranquil surroundings. Dinner will be served at your hotel, and you will spend the night, ready to explore the beautiful beaches and attractions of Zanzibar.

Day 7: Zanzibar

After an early breakfast, you'll embark on a thrilling Blue Safari in Zanzibar, a popular excursion that takes you on a dhow boat ride to nearby islands like Kwale Island. Here, you can explore stunning coral reefs, snorkel with vibrant tropical fish, and enjoy a fresh seafood lunch served on the boat as you cruise through the crystal-clear waters of the Indian Ocean. It's a day filled with adventure and relaxation in a breathtaking natural setting. You'll enjoy full board accommodation, with meals provided throughout the day. After your exciting day, you'll return to your lodge for a relaxing dinner and overnight stay, soaking in the peaceful atmosphere of the island.

Day 8: Zanzibar

You will begin with a fascinating Stone Town exploration. Known for its rich history and stunning architecture, Stone Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site filled with narrow alleys, bustling markets, and beautiful historical buildings. During your tour, you'll visit significant landmarks such as the Lutheran Church, where you can learn about the historical and cultural significance of the church in Zanzibar's diverse religious landscape. After exploring the town's vibrant streets, you will have some time to soak in the sights and sounds of this unique town. In the late afternoon, you will transfer back to your hotel, where you can unwind and enjoy a delicious dinner. Your day will end with an overnight stay at your beach resort, providing a restful end to an enriching day of exploration.

Day 9: Zanzibar

You'll enjoy a leisurely day of relaxation, taking in the tranquil atmosphere and beautiful surroundings at your lodge. In the late afternoon, you will embark on a sunset dhow activity, where you'll sail on a traditional wooden dhow boat, watching the stunning colors of the sunset over the Indian Ocean. This peaceful and picturesque experience offers a perfect way to end your day. After the dhow excursion, you'll return to your lodge for a restful overnight stay.

Day 10: Travel Day

After an early morning check-out, your driver will transfer you to Zanzibar International Airport for your international flight onward. We hope you had a wonderful and memorable trip, and we wish you a safe and pleasant journey ahead!
