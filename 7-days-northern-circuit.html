7-Day Northern Tanzania Safari Adventure

Embark on an unforgettable 7-day safari through Tanzania's most iconic northern circuit parks. This comprehensive journey takes you from the vibrant town of Arusha to Lake Manyara National Park, the magnificent Serengeti plains, the breathtaking Ngorongoro Crater, and the wildlife-rich Tarangire National Park.

Experience exceptional game viewing opportunities with chances to spot the Big Five (lion, leopard, rhino, elephant, and buffalo), along with giraffes, zebras, cheetahs, and the famous wildebeest migration in the Serengeti. Enjoy comfortable accommodations, expert guided game drives, and immersive cultural experiences on this perfect introduction to Tanzania's wildlife wonders.

Day 1: Arrival Day

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) at TBA, you will be warmly welcomed by your guide and transferred to your accommodation. After check-in, you'll have time to relax and settle in. In the evening, enjoy a delicious dinner followed by a pre-safari briefing to prepare you for the exciting journey ahead. Overnight stay at your lodge.

Day 2: Arusha – Lake Manyara – Karatu

We depart early in the morning from Arusha and head towards Lake Manyara National Park, covering approximately 125 kilometers in about 2 hours. Upon arrival, we embark on game drives and exploration of the park, renowned for its lush scenery, tree-climbing lions, and diverse birdlife. A packed lunch will be enjoyed amidst the beautiful surroundings of the park. In the late afternoon, after the game drive, we proceed to the nearby town of Mto wa Mbu, located just 5 minutes from the Manyara gate. Here, we immerse ourselves in cultural exploration, visiting local farms, sampling traditional cuisine, and learning about the rich cultural diversity of the town, home to over 120 different tribes. Later, we continue our journey, ascending the scenic Rift Valley escarpment wall as we make our way to the Crater Highlands. The day concludes with dinner and an overnight stay at a lodge or camp, surrounded by the stunning highland landscapes.

Day 3: Karatu – Central Serengeti

After an early breakfast, we depart from the Crater Highlands and make our way to the central part of Serengeti National Park. The journey covers approximately 200 kilometers and takes around 5 to 6 hours, including game viewing along the way. Our route passes through the stunning Ngorongoro Conservation Area, offering breathtaking landscapes and a chance to spot wildlife even before reaching Serengeti. With packed lunches provided, we continue the drive as the scenery transitions from the highlands to the endless plains of the Serengeti. Upon entering the park, we begin game drives en route, witnessing the incredible diversity of wildlife that the Serengeti is famous for, including large herds of wildebeest, zebras, predators, and a variety of bird species. By late afternoon, we arrive at your special camp or lodge in the central Serengeti, where you will enjoy dinner and an overnight stay under the vast African skies.

Day 4: Central Serengeti

You will spend the full day in the central Serengeti, exploring this iconic and expansive national park known for its incredible wildlife and dramatic landscapes. After breakfast, we will embark on a morning game drive, where you'll have the opportunity to witness the rich diversity of animals, including the famous "Big Five" — lions, elephants, leopards, buffaloes, and rhinos. Depending on the time of year, you may also have the chance to see the incredible wildebeest migration if you're visiting during that season. A packed lunch will be provided, and you can enjoy it either in the park or during a stop at a scenic viewpoint, immersing yourself in the serenity of the Serengeti. In the late afternoon, we will continue with another game drive, exploring different parts of the park and spotting more wildlife. After a fulfilling day of adventure, we will return to our accommodation in the Serengeti for dinner and overnight, where you can relax and reflect on the day's experiences.

Day 5: Central Serengeti – Ngorongoro Crater – Karatu

After breakfast, we depart from the Serengeti and head towards the Ngorongoro Crater, covering approximately 145 kilometers in about 3 to 4 hours. Upon arrival, we descend 600 meters into the crater floor, one of the world's largest unbroken calderas and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. With your packed lunch, you will enjoy an unforgettable game drive inside the crater, often referred to as the "Eighth Wonder of the World." The crater is home to a remarkable concentration of wildlife, including the Big Five—lion, elephant, buffalo, rhino, and leopard—along with countless other species thriving in its unique ecosystem. In the late afternoon, after ample exploration and wildlife viewing, we ascend back up the crater walls and return to the Crater Highlands. Here, you will have dinner and spend the night at your lodge or camp, surrounded by the cool highland air and scenic views.

Day 6: Karatu – Tarangire

After breakfast, we depart for Tarangire National Park, a drive of approximately 150 kilometers taking around 3 hours. Known for its iconic baobab trees, vast savannah landscapes, and large herds of elephants, Tarangire offers a truly unique safari experience. With packed lunch provided, we spend the full day enjoying game drives throughout the park, exploring its diverse habitats and observing a wide variety of wildlife, including lions, giraffes, zebras, and numerous bird species drawn to the Tarangire River. In the late afternoon, after a fulfilling day of exploration, we proceed to our special camp where a delicious dinner awaits. You'll spend the night under the starlit African sky, surrounded by the sounds of nature.

Day 7: Full Day Tarangire - Arusha

We wake up early in the heart of Tarangire National Park, ready to join private game drives and further exploration of this remarkable park. The early morning hours offer the best chances to witness wildlife at its most active—elephants moving through the misty savannah, predators on the hunt, and various bird species starting their day. As we traverse different parts of the park, you'll have opportunities to soak in the tranquil beauty of Tarangire unique landscapes and spot any wildlife you may have missed the day before. In the late afternoon, we check out of the park and begin our drive back to Arusha, covering approximately 120 kilometers in about 2.5 to 3 hours. Upon arrival, we will transfer you to Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) in time to catch your international flight onward, marking the end of your unforgettable safari adventure.
