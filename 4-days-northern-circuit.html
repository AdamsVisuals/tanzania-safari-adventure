4-Day Northern Tanzania Safari Adventure

Embark on an unforgettable 4-day safari through Tanzania's most iconic northern circuit parks. This carefully curated journey takes you from the vibrant town of Arusha to the wildlife-rich Tarangire National Park, the diverse Lake Manyara ecosystem, and culminates with the breathtaking Ngorongoro Crater.

Experience exceptional game viewing opportunities with chances to spot the Big Five (lion, leopard, rhino, elephant, and buffalo), along with giraffes, zebras, hippos, and the famous tree-climbing lions of Lake Manyara. Enjoy comfortable accommodations, expert guided game drives, and immersive cultural experiences on this perfect introduction to Tanzania's wildlife wonders.

Day 1: Arrive Arusha

You will arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), where you will be warmly greeted by your dedicated safari guide. They will assist you with your transfer to your accommodation, ensuring a smooth and comfortable journey. Once at your lodge or hotel, you'll have time to relax and settle in before enjoying a delicious dinner. Afterward, you'll have an overnight stay, allowing you to rest and recharge for the exciting safari ahead. In the evening, there will be a pre-safari briefing, where your guide will provide essential information about the safari, covering safety protocols, the upcoming itinerary, and what to expect during the adventure.

Day 2: Arusha – Tarangire – Karatu

Early in the morning after breakfast, we will depart for Tarangire National Park for a full day of game drives and exploration. Tarangire is one of Tanzania's largest and most beautiful parks, famous for its ancient baobab trees and large herds of African elephants. The park is home to a wide variety of wildlife including lions, giraffes, zebras, and many bird species, offering an incredible safari experience. The drive from Arusha to Tarangire is approximately 120 km, taking about 2 hours. You will enjoy your packed lunch inside the park, surrounded by the stunning scenery and wildlife. In the late afternoon, we will check out of the park and drive towards Karatu for dinner and overnight. The drive from Tarangire to Karatu is around 100 km, taking approximately 2 to 2.5 hours, passing through picturesque landscapes, local villages, and along the dramatic Rift Valley escarpment.

Day 3: Karatu – Lake Manyara – Karatu

Early in the morning after breakfast, we will drive about 30 minutes to Lake Manyara National Park for a full day of game drives and exploration. This beautiful park is known for its diverse habitats, from groundwater forests to open savannahs, and is home to a wide variety of wildlife, including elephants, giraffes, hippos, and the famous tree-climbing lions. After enjoying a picnic lunch in the park, we will head to the nearby town of Mto wa Mbu, located right at the edge of the park. Here, you will be immersed in authentic African culture—Mto wa Mbu is unique as it is home to representatives from all of Tanzania's 120 tribes, living together in harmony. During your cultural tour, you'll walk through local farms, learn about the traditional irrigation systems, visit the bustling local market, and witness the artisans at work with their crafting activities. To complete the experience, you'll get a chance to cook and taste traditional Tanzanian cuisine. After this enriching day, we will drive back to your lodge for dinner and overnight.

Day 4: Karatu - Ngorongoro Crater - Arusha

After breakfast, taking picnic lunches with you and drive to Ngorongoro crater descend down to the crater floor for a full day of tracking game with your guide. The crater is 610m deep and 260km² wide, making it the largest Caldera in the world. The crater contains all the big five animals (lions, elephants, rhinos, leopard and buffaloes), hippos, cheetah, antelopes and spotted hyena. Also in the crater you can see different species of birds. And some animals such as velvet monkey, jackal, foxes, rock and tree hyraxes, warthog, gnu, zebra and hare. In the late afternoon, after your game drive and exploration in Ngorongoro Crater, you will ascend from the caldera and begin your drive back to Arusha. The journey from Ngorongoro to Arusha covers approximately 190 km and takes about 4 hours, passing through scenic landscapes and local villages. Upon arrival in Arusha, you will be transferred directly to Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) for your onward international flight.
